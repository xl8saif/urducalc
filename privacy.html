مقامی ذخیرہ

زبان اور ہندسوں کی طرز جیسی ترجیحات صرف آپ کے ڈیوائس پر مقامی طور پر محفوظ کی جا سکتی ہیں۔ یہ معلومات کسی سرور یا تیسرے فریق کو نہیں بھیجی جاتیں۔

وزٹر کاؤنٹر اور تیسرے فریق

اکاؤنٹس اور اشتہارات

ایپ میں اکاؤنٹ بنانے کی ضرورت نہیں اور اردو کیلکولیٹر خود کوئی اشتہاری SDK استعمال نہیں کرتا۔ وزٹر کاؤنٹر الگ بیرونی سروس ہے اور اس کی درخواستیں کاؤنٹر سروس کے سرور تک جاتی ہیں۔

ڈیٹا شیئرنگ، تحفظ اور حذف

Privacy Policy — Urdu Calculator

App: Urdu Calculator

Developer: WARAQ Enterprises, Gilgit

Data Collection

Urdu Calculator does not collect, upload, or transmit personal, sensitive, or financial information to any server. Calculations, age calculations, Zakat calculations, and unit conversions are performed locally on your device.

Local Storage

Preferences such as language and digit style may be stored locally on your device. These preferences are not sent to any server or third party.

Visitor Counter and Third Party Service

The website uses an external visitor counter to display an aggregate visitor count. The counter is provided by Besucherzaehler.gratis. According to that service, visitors are counted using IP addresses on a daily basis; IP addresses are not permanently stored, and the counter does not use cookies. The counter provider's own privacy policy and terms also apply.

Accounts and Advertising

The app does not require an account and Urdu Calculator itself does not use an advertising SDK. The visitor counter is a separate third-party service, and its requests are sent to the counter provider's servers.

Data Sharing, Security, and Deletion

Because the app does not collect personal data on a server, WARAQ Enterprises does not sell, rent, share, or provide personal data to third parties. Local preferences can be removed by clearing the app or site data through the device/browser settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact WARAQ Enterprises through the Issues section of the Urdu Calculator repository:

https://github.com/xl8saif/urdu-calculator/issues
